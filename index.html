Who We Are

AGHODEST COMPANY LIMITED is a leading environmental consulting firm specializing in environmental management, applied geophysics, research, and project management services for private and government institutions worldwide.

We are committed to sustainability and innovation, delivering tailored solutions — including water quality monitoring, remediation, environmental audits, and geophysical investigations — to tackle complex challenges in regions such as Toledo, Ohio, the Maumee River watershed, western Lake Erie, and beyond.
